# Will You Go Out With Me? 💗

A cute single-page romantic website.

## Run it
Just open `index.html` in a browser.

## Customize it
- Change the main question in `index.html`.
- Replace the success message with your own inside the `.success` section.
- Change fonts/colors in `style.css`.
- The playful No button and heart animation are controlled by `script.js`.

For a personal touch, replace the text with your girlfriend's name and your own date/message.
